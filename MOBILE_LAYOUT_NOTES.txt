Bodega Wars v0.1.4 mobile layout notes

Changed by request after mobile testing.

What changed

The End Day button is now easy to find.
A quick action bar appears under the player stats on every active game screen.
The quick action bar includes End Day, Market, Safe, Travel, and Dark Terminals.
On phones, End Day becomes the large full-width top button in the quick action bar.

The mobile navigation is now always visible as a horizontal scroll row.
The player no longer has to open a hidden menu just to move between screens.

Mobile layout improvements

Stats stay in a compact two-column grid on phones.
Buttons are larger and easier to tap.
Inputs and dropdowns are full width on small screens.
Hero and screen images are shorter on phones so the game controls show sooner.
Tables still scroll sideways when needed, but the touch areas are larger.

Developer note

Keep the game web-app first.
Do not re-add private/local place names.
Do not bury End Day at the bottom of a page again.
