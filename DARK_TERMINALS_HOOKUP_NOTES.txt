Bodega Wars Dark Terminals hookup update

Version 0.1.3 changes Dark Terminals from a vendor market into three fictional hookup people.

The design goal is simple:
Street buying is instant but costs more and raises street risk.
Dark Terminals buying is cheaper but requires fake coins, waiting, and risk.
