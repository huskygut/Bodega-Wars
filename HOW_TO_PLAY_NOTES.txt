Bodega Wars v0.1.5 How to Play notes

Changed by request after mobile testing.

What changed

Added a How to Play screen to the game.

The How to Play screen explains:

Buy low and sell high.
Use the Safe House to protect cash and goods.
Watch Heat, Turf Pressure, and Suspicion.
Use Crypto Carl for the fake coin ladder.
Use Dark Terminals for lower-price fictional hookups with risk.
Buy the bodega to unlock Register Cash and daily profit.
Use End Day to advance prices, shipments, bills, interest, and events.

Where it appears

Main Street title screen has a How to Play button.
The left/mobile navigation has a How to Play option.
Settings has a How to Play card with an Open How to Play button.
New Game opens the How to Play screen first so new players are not dumped straight into the market.

Mobile note

The tutorial uses stacked cards on phones and keeps the Start Playing button large enough to tap easily.

Developer note

Keep the tutorial short and beginner-friendly.
Update it whenever major game systems change.
Do not add personal local place names.
