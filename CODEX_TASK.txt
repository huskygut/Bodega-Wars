Privacy rule
Do not add personal local places, hometowns, addresses, or locations tied to the creator. Use only generic fictional locations or large well-known cities.


Dark Terminals hookup system
Keep Dark Terminals fictional and parody-only.
Dark Terminals should use three hookup people, not real sites and not a generic market.
Each hookup should have a different price, quality range, heat risk, delay, ghost chance, and bunk-good chance.
Hookup prices should usually be lower than the current street price because the player already pays coin conversion fees and delivery risk.
Do not add real dark web steps, real marketplace names, real crypto wallets, or real illegal purchasing details.
