Bodega Wars v0.1.7 Money System Notes

This update improves the fake economy so Dark Terminals has a real gameplay advantage over street dealers.

Changes

Dark Terminals advantage

Dark Terminals now uses wholesale hookup pricing instead of feeling like another market.

The three fictional hookups are designed around different tradeoffs:

Uncle Modem
Deepest discount
Accepts FraggleCoin
Highest bunk chance
Medium heat
Slow delivery

Packet Patty
Balanced discount
Accepts OnionCoin
Better quality than Uncle Modem
Lower risk
Fast delivery

Shadow Sal
Premium hookup
Accepts DevilCoin
Still under street after expected coin conversion fees
Lowest heat
Lowest bunk chance

Bulk discounts

Dark Terminals orders now get additional quantity discounts:

10 or more units gives 5 percent bulk discount
25 or more units gives 10 percent bulk discount
50 or more units gives 15 percent bulk discount

Why use Dark Terminals?

The street dealers are instant, but they use street prices and create Hot Cash and street pressure.

Dark Terminals requires fake coins and delivery time, but gives:

Lower unit costs
Bulk discounts
Potentially lower heat
Shipments delivered to the Safe House
Better margins when the player buys fake coins low and spends them after the price rises

Risks still remain:

Ghosted order chance
Bunk goods chance
Delayed delivery
Coin conversion fees
Coin price volatility

Crypto report

A Daily Crypto Report now appears on the Crypto Carl screen.

It shows:

Old price
New price
Daily percent change
A short market note for FraggleCoin, OnionCoin, and DevilCoin
Recent report history

The report is generated each time the player ends a day.

Safety note

All currencies, markets, hookups, vendors, goods, and events are fictional parody content. No real crypto wallets, real marketplaces, real dark web instructions, or real-world transaction steps are included.


Street Hookups

The old single street market is now a three-person street hookup system.

Cheder Bob
Lowest buy prices
Highest bunk chance
Moderate heat and turf pressure
Good for cheap flips when the player accepts risk

Crazy Eyes
Normal buy prices
Best sell payouts
Higher heat and turf pressure
Good when the player wants cash fast

Walter Whiteboard
Highest buy prices
Lowest bunk chance
Lower heat and turf pressure
Good when the player wants safer instant street buys

Why this improves the money loop

Street Hookups are instant and easy, but inconsistent.
Dark Terminals requires fake coin conversion and delivery time, but has wholesale pricing, bulk discounts, lower unit costs, and delivery to the Safe House.
Crypto trading remains useful because buying coins low and spending them later improves terminal margins.
